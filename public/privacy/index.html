What happens to what you share.

Last updated 17 October 2026. Co-Pilot is operated by Phase 2 Labs LLC.

What we collect

What you type into a form on this site: your email, plus anything else you choose to add, such as your name, type of business or your biggest AI question. When you book a call, the scheduling page collects your name, email and the time you pick. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity show us how the site is used, and they set cookies.

How we use it

To reply to you, to tell you when membership opens, and to write briefings that fit your business. We don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone to market to you.

Where it goes

Form answers are emailed to me and kept in my own systems. Bookings go on my calendar. Email delivery, scheduling and analytics run on service providers who handle it on our behalf.

Your choices

Email shawn@energiacres.com to see what we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings.
