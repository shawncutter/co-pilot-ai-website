## What it is
When you reserve a spot, your deposit holds your place in line for the cohort. It's a
**refundable reservation** — not a ticket, and not a purchase of goods or services.

## The amounts
- **$50 — Lock Your Spot:** holds your place in line.
- **$100 — Priority Line:** moves you higher in the selection order and gives first
  pick of format and logistics.

Priority affects **order and timing only** — it does **not** guarantee admission.

## It's fully refundable if…
1. the cohort doesn't reach its builder threshold and the event doesn't run;
2. you apply and aren't selected; or
3. you change your mind and request a refund **before the cohort is confirmed**.

Pre-confirmation refunds are returned in full within **[X business days]**.

## If you're selected
Once you accept your spot, your deposit is **credited 100% toward your entry fee**.
Any remaining balance is due **[by date / on these terms]**.

## Reserving isn't admission
Selection is based on your application and fit. A deposit reserves your **place in
line**, not a seat in the room.

## If the details change
Final pricing, dates, city, and format are confirmed the moment we hit the threshold.
If any of these change **materially** from what you signed up for, you may withdraw
for a full refund.

## Payments
Processed securely by **Stripe**. We never see or store your card details.
