From idea to scored venture in 48 hours.

Every participant submits ideas before the event. The best ideas enter the pool. Pods form. Prototypes ship. Evaluators score. Winners advance.

Submit Ideas

1-3 ideas per applicant. Pre-vetted for buildability, specificity, and AI-native potential before admission.

Curated Cohort

15-25 builders selected for skill, domain, and execution track record. No tourists. Every seat earns its place.

Fluid pods coalesce around the highest-leverage ideas. Cross-functional by design. Ship-oriented from minute one.
